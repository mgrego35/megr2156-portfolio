## Analyze
**Task A: Portfolio Analysis**

Portfolio 1: https://www.alexandre-allonas.fr/

This portfolio is easily traversable, it has a header for each major section, and for each project has a small explanation with links to "more information" or source codes for the project. Reproducibility is what this portfolio lacks. The projects on this website are given a small description, to introduce it, but do not go deep enough into each project. It gives mostly the beginning and the end, without giving any idea into the processes in between other than a "tools used" section for each. The tone of the portfolio is highly businesslike. If it were not for the lack of details on the projects, this would be a perfect portfolio to show to a possible employer.

Portfolio 2: https://github.com/ikbal-nayem/ikbal-nayem

This portfolio is easy to navigate. It is very concise and has a relatively low amount of information. This portfolio also has very low reproducibility. Unfortunately, it as well has more of a beginning to end idea rather than explaining the process. It has a solid amount of information on what applications, programming languages, etc. were used to complete the projects included. The overall tone is slightly less than formal due to the emojis used, it is not something to hand to an employer.
