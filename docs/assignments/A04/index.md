## Feature 1 Design

The first thing required was to find the minimum width of feature of the cross section using the known variables shown. I chose the height of feature 1 to be 10 mm and the length to be 40 mm. This was based on a safety factor of 3 and yield strength, and then also max deflection.

The final width was 28 mm based on a rounded answer from the minimum which was found from the max deflection.
